Admission Form Reader

Reads the typed information from a photo of the admission form. The form is split into left/right column images before OCR, since the original layout confuses line-based text reading. The Auth number field is outlined in red if the OCR found it blank — tap in to fill it manually if needed. The account number is handwritten on these forms and gets typed in later, directly in the filing app — not here.
